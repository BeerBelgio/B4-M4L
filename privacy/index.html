PRIVACY

B4~M4L provides documentation and support for BeerBelgio Max for Live devices. This page explains how information is handled when you visit or contact the operator.

WHO RUNS THIS WEBSITE

Matteo Belgiovine (BeerBelgio) based in Turin, Italy, operates this website. For any requests: matteo.sonodgtl@gmail.com.

HOSTING

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit a GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs IP addresses for security, including visits from people who are not signed in. GitHub controls those hosting logs under its own privacy statement; the operator does not independently control their retention. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s privacy statement.

ANALYTICS AND TRACKING

This build contains no analytics, advertising pixels, profiling scripts, accounts, contact forms or third-party embeds. Fonts and illustrations are included locally. The site code does not set cookies. Browser preferences are used only for interface functionality, without cross-site tracking.

LANGUAGE AND LAYOUT PREFERENCES

The HELP language is saved as b4m4l.language in localStorage, where it can remain between visits until changed or cleared. If localStorage is unavailable, sessionStorage is used when possible. A language code may also appear in page URLs.

Homepage positions and sizes are saved as b4m4l.hero.session.v1.main.layout4 in sessionStorage. RESET removes the current saved composition. A previous layout3 value may remain from an earlier build; this build leaves it untouched. The translation notice uses b4m4l.translation-notice.de, .es or .fr in sessionStorage to appear once per language and tab session.

These values are not sent to the operator. Session values normally end with the tab session, although browser session restoration can preserve them. You can clear website storage through your browser. Editing and reading still work if storage is blocked; preferences may then last only while the page remains open.

EXTERNAL LINKS

Links to Ko-fi, maxforlive.com, BeerBelgio’s other pages, the INDEX HTML project and other references take you to separate websites. Their privacy notices apply there. This site does not process purchases or device downloads. Opening an external link does not itself purchase anything.

SUPPORT EMAIL

REPORT AN ISSUE opens your email application with a suggested message that you may edit. The website does not send it, attach files or collect diagnostics automatically. If you send an email, the operator receives your address, message and any information you choose to include, to assess and answer your enquiry. This correspondence is processed on the basis of the operator’s legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). You are free not to contact the operator; without relevant information a request may be impossible to answer.

The operator uses Gmail/Google for receiving and storing support email and is the only person who accesses the support mailbox. Your email provider also handles delivery. See Google’s privacy policy.

Support correspondence is kept in Gmail while reasonably needed to respond to enquiries and handle related follow-up, or where continued retention is justified by applicable legal obligations. There is no fixed automatic deletion schedule. You can request deletion at the contact above; such requests are assessed under applicable law.

PROVIDERS AND INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

GitHub and Google may process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Their privacy notices explain their processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards. This notice does not promise that all hosting or email data stays in Italy or the EU. Refer to the providers’ linked privacy notices for details about their data processing and safeguards.

YOUR RIGHTS

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Send requests to the operator’s contact above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
